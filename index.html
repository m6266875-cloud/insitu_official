About Us

Insitudesigns is a London based studio working across furniture, (art)itecture and design. Each piece starts as a simple form and is refined until nothing extra is left.

Pieces are drawn part by part, then rendered in real interiors so material, light and scale can be judged. The Spotlight Series revisits chairs by Finn Juhl and Hans J. Wegner.
